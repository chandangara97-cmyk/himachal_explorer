READY-TO-PASTE BREADCRUMB MARKUP (one block per page)
============================================================
Paste the block into the page's <head> (or just above </body>). One BreadcrumbList per page.
The visible breadcrumb on the page should say the same thing.
Assumes /dist_master.html is your 'Districts' page. If not, change that URL.

<script type="application/ld+json">
