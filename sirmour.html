About Sirmour

Sirmour is Himachal's southernmost and least touristed accessible district. Renuka Lake (HP's largest natural lake) is sacred to Lord Parashurama. The Shivalik fossil park contains million-year-old vertebrate fossils. Churdhar (3,647 m) is the highest peak of the outer Himalayas.

Top Places to Visit in Sirmour

  • Renuka LakeHP's largest lake. Renuka fair in November. Adjoining mini-zoo.
  • Churdhar Peak3,647 m. Highest peak of outer Himalaya. Shirgul Maharaj temple at top.
  • Suketi Fossil ParkAsia's first fossil park. Life-size replicas of Shivalik fauna (5–8 million years old).
  • Paonta SahibHistoric Gurudwara on Yamuna. Guru Gobind Singh wrote here.
  • Nahan17th-century planned town. Rani Tal garden, Jagannath temple.
