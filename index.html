Frequently Asked Questions

Best packages from Delhi?

Manali Classic 5N/6D from ₹8,500, Shimla–Manali 6N/7D from ₹12,500, Spiti Circuit 7N/8D from ₹17,500. Call +91-7018138847.

Best time to visit?

Mar–Jun for Shimla & Kullu. Jun–Sep for Spiti & Lahaul. Oct–Nov for Kinnaur apple harvest. Dec–Feb for snow in Manali & Kufri.

ILP needed for Spiti or Kinnaur?

No ILP for Spiti. Kinnaur inner areas (Chitkul, Nako, Pooh) require one — from SDM offices at Shimla, Rampur or Rekong Peo. We assist with permits.

Manali tour cost from Delhi?

5-night Manali: approx ₹8,500–₹12,500/person for 2–4 travellers including cab, 3-star hotel & sightseeing. Peak season 10–15% higher.

Can I customise my itinerary?

Yes. All packages are fully customisable for solo, couples, families & groups. WhatsApp Himachal Explorer for an instant quote.
