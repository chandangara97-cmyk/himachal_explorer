About Kinnaur

Kinnaur borders Tibet. Apple, apricot and grape orchards cling to sheer cliffsides. Chitkul is the last inhabited village on the Indo-Tibetan border road. Kalpa offers the finest views of Kinnaur Kailash (6,050 m). A permit is required for areas beyond Jangi.
