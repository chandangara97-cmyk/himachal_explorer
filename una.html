About Una

Una is Himachal's gateway from Punjab. Chintpurni temple is one of the 51 Shakti Peethas and receives millions of devotees annually. The Amb temples are early medieval stone temples in excellent condition. Pong Dam is a Ramsar wetland hosting bar-headed geese in winter.
