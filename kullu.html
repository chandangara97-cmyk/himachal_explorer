About Kullu

Kullu district is the adventure capital of Himachal Pradesh. The Kullu Valley, carved by the Beas river, stretches from Larji gorge to Rohtang Pass. Manali is the gateway to Leh-Ladakh and Spiti. Famous for Kullu Dussehra festival, world-class trekking, skiing, river rafting and paragliding.
