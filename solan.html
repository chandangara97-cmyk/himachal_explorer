About Solan

Solan is closest to Chandigarh. Kasauli is one of India's best-preserved colonial hill stations. The Kasauli Brewery (1820) is Asia's oldest functioning brewery. Solan is called the Mushroom City of India. Chail has the world's highest cricket ground (2,250 m).
