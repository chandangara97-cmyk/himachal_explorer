About Hamirpur

Hamirpur has the highest literacy rate in Himachal Pradesh. Sujanpur Tira has a historic fort and riverside ghats on the Beas. Deotsidh (Baba Balak Nath) is one of the most important pilgrimage sites drawing millions from Punjab.
