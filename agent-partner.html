Questions from agents

Who can join?

Independent travel consultants and small agencies based outside Himachal Pradesh. You do not need a registered company to apply.

Why is approval needed?

Agent prices are for partners only. We approve each application before the agent price list is unlocked.

How is my price calculated?

Your price is the public price minus your commission. The difference is what you keep when a customer books at the public price.

Will my customer see the agent price?

No. The quote you copy or share on WhatsApp shows only the public price.
