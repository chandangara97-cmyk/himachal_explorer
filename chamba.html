About Chamba

Chamba is Himachal's most underrated district. The old Chamba town has ancient temples dating back 1,000+ years. Dalhousie is a former British cantonment with colonial charm. Khajjiar is the Mini Switzerland of India — a circular meadow ringed by deodars.

Top Places to Visit in Chamba

  • DalhousieFormer British cantonment at 2,036 m. Churches, cottages, pine forests.
  • KhajjiarMini Switzerland of India. 1.5 km circular meadow, deodar ring, lake.
  • Chamba TownAncient capital. Lakshmi Narayan temple complex. Chaugan ground.
  • Manimahesh LakeSacred lake at 4,080 m below Manimahesh Kailash (5,653 m). Annual yatra.
  • BharmaurFormer capital. Chaurasi temples complex — 84 ancient shrines.
