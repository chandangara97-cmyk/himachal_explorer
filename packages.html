Best Time to Visit Himachal Pradesh

March–June: Shimla, Kullu Valley, Dharamshala — blooming rhododendrons, comfortable temperatures. June–September: Spiti & Lahaul — roads open post-snowmelt, best for high-altitude circuits. October–November: Kinnaur apple harvest, crisp skies for photography. December–February: Snow experiences in Manali, Kufri, Solang Valley.
