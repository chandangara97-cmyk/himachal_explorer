Explore All 12 Districts of Himachal Pradesh

Himachal Pradesh has twelve districts, each with different landscapes, attractions, cultures, roads, climates and travel experiences. Explore district-specific information before choosing your route.

  • Shimla district — colonial heritage, hill stations, viewpoints and family holidays.
  • Bilaspur — reservoirs, temples and Himalayan foothill landscapes.
  • Chamba — heritage, valleys, temples, Khajjiar and high mountain routes.
  • Kangra — Dharamshala, McLeod Ganj, monasteries, tea gardens and Bir Billing.
  • Kinnaur — mountain villages, apple orchards, sacred peaks and scenic valleys.
  • Kullu — river valleys, temples, adventure activities and access to Manali.
  • Lahaul and Spiti — cold deserts, monasteries, high passes and remote landscapes.
  • Mandi — temples, rivers, lakes and important travel connections.
  • Sirmaur — Renuka Lake, forests, heritage and lower Himalayan scenery.
  • Solan — forests, hill towns, temples and convenient road access.
  • Una — foothill attractions, temples and gateway routes.
  • Hamirpur — cultural sites, temples and central Himachal connections.

Frequently Asked Questions About Himachal Pradesh Tourism

What are the best places to visit in Himachal Pradesh?

Popular choices include Shimla, Manali, Dharamshala, Dalhousie, Spiti, Kinnaur, Kasol and Bir Billing. The right destination depends on season, available days and travel interests.

How many days are needed for a Himachal Pradesh trip?

A short trip may take 3 to 5 days, while broader circuits covering multiple valleys or high-altitude regions may require 7 to 15 days or more.

Which is the best time to visit Himachal Pradesh?

Different regions suit different seasons. Lower and mid-hill destinations are commonly visited throughout much of the year, while high-altitude routes depend heavily on snow, road and weather conditions.
