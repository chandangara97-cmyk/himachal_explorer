About Mandi

Mandi is called the Varanasi of the Hills for its 81 ancient temples. Prashar Lake — a high-altitude lake with a 3-storey pagoda — is one of Himachal's most mystical destinations. Rewalsar Lake is sacred to Buddhists, Hindus and Sikhs simultaneously.
