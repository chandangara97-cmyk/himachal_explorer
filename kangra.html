About Kangra

Kangra is Himachal's most populated district. Dharamshala is home to the Dalai Lama and the Tibetan government-in-exile. Bir-Billing is the paragliding capital of Asia, hosting world record flights. The ancient Kangra Fort, Brajeshwari temple and tea gardens of Palampur make this a district of extraordinary diversity — from Tibetan Buddhist culture to Hindu pilgrimage to adventure sports.

Top Places to Visit in Kangra

  • Dharamshala / McLeod GanjHome of Dalai Lama. Namgyal Monastery, Tibetan market, café culture.
  • Bir BillingAsia's paragliding capital. World record flights. Take-off at Billing (2,400 m).
  • PalampurHimachal's tea capital. Lush Neugal Khad gorge. Colonial era tea estates.
  • Kangra FortOne of India's oldest and largest forts. 4,000+ years of history. ASI protected.
  • Triund Trek9 km trek from McLeod Ganj. Panoramic Dhauladhar views. Camping at top.
