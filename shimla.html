About Shimla

Shimla served as the summer capital of British India from 1864. The district blends Victorian architecture — Viceregal Lodge, Christ Church, Gaiety Theatre — with dense cedar forests. Beyond the city: Narkanda for skiing, Rampur for tribal culture, Chail for the world's highest cricket ground at 2,250 m.
