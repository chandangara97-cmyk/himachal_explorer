About Bilaspur

Bilaspur's old town was submerged when the Bhakra Dam created Gobind Sagar Lake in 1954. Gobind Sagar (540 km²) is one of India's largest man-made lakes and a major water sports centre. Naina Devi temple on a ridge overlooking the lake is one of the most important Shakti Peethas in northern India.

Top Places to Visit in Bilaspur

  • Gobind Sagar Lake540 km² reservoir. Boat rides, water sports, angling. Stunning sunset point.
  • Naina Devi TempleHilltop Shakti Peetha at 1219 m. Ropeway. Panoramic lake views.
  • Bhakra DamOne of India's highest gravity dams (226 m). Nehru called it 'New Temple of India'.
  • Markand TempleAncient Shiva temple complex on Gobind Sagar bank. Carved stone sculptures.
  • SwarghatConfluence point of Satluj into Gobind Sagar. Scenic river beach.
